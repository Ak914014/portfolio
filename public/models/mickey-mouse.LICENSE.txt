Adaptations for this portfolio: converted to self-contained glTF/GLB, reduced
subdivision for web rendering, retained the deformation skeleton, and converted
the creator's packed baked color textures to standard PBR materials. Scene
helpers, embedded UI scripts, cameras and lights are excluded from the GLB.
Runtime character scale, idle pose and movement are adapted for the portfolio.
